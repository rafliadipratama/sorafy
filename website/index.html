FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, factual answers without technical obfuscation or corporate spin.

Where does SoraFy get its music? +

SoraFy indexes and streams audio from public, open-web audio repositories including YouTube Music and community databases, selecting highest-bitrate Opus and AAC audio streams. It does not run illegal torrent swarms or host proprietary copyrighted files on private servers.

Is SoraFy safe and privacy-friendly? +

Yes. SoraFy is completely open source under the GPL-3.0 license. The entire codebase is auditable on GitHub. There are no tracking scripts, analytics cookies, device fingerprinting, or telemetry beacons. Your listening history and saved playlists are stored in a local JSON database on your computer.

How does playlist importing work without logging in? +

SoraFy parses public metadata (track titles, artists, and album order) from Spotify and Apple Music URLs using public embed APIs. It then resolves each track against high-fidelity audio streams in seconds, without requiring you to share Spotify passwords or OAuth tokens.

What is the Music Player Daemon (MPD) protocol socket? +

MPD is the standard Linux audio protocol supported by terminal clients like ncmpcpp, mpc, and window manager status bars. SoraFy runs an embedded MPD daemon on port 6600, allowing power users to send commands like mpc next, mpc toggle, or control playback via keyboard hotkeys.

How does the SoraFy Jam phone remote work? +

When you click the mobile icon in SoraFy, the app serves a lightweight local web controller on port 4120. Scan the QR code with your smartphone on the same local Wi-Fi network—no app store download is required on your phone.

Can I listen to music completely offline? +

Yes. Songs you stream are automatically stored in your local LRU cache quota. You can also right-click any track and select "Download Track (MP3)" to save permanent audio files to your computer using the built-in yt-dlp companion.
